# Clean Article Specification

## Fidelity

- Preserve the complete substantive article in its original order.
- Do not summarize, shorten, paraphrase, fact-check, or add commentary.
- Preserve headings, quotations, lists, emphasis, captions, footnotes, and meaningful hyperlinks.
- Do not invent missing text, author names, dates, captions, or section titles.

## Remove

- Site navigation, menus, advertisements, tracking elements, and cookie notices.
- Subscription, registration, newsletter, donation, and application prompts.
- Sharing controls, reaction controls, comments, recommendations, and related stories that are not part of the article.
- Duplicate titles, duplicate paragraphs, repeated captions, and other extraction artifacts.

## Metadata and structure

- Use the article's actual headline rather than the browser-tab title.
- Preserve the subtitle, author names, publication, date, and canonical URL when supported by the source.
- Use semantic structure and ordinary paragraphs without reproducing website styling.
- Preserve section headings but do not create new ones.
